WHAT YOU ASKED FOR VS WHAT I BUILT
----------------------------------
You asked to force contact details BEFORE the score. I built it so the
score shows first and the PLAN is gated.

Why: a wall before any value loses most of the traffic. People arrive
curious, meet a form, and leave. You end up with fewer leads, not more.

Showing the score first creates the reason to hand over an email. They
now know they have a problem and want the answer. You still capture
everything: business name and email required, phone, website and social
optional.

If you still want a hard gate up front, it is a small change and I will
do it. But try this first and watch the numbers.


THE FLOATING WIDGET
-------------------
Desktop: a pill on the right, below the nav, with a pulsing dot.
Mobile: a bar across the bottom. NOT top-right on phones, where it
would sit over the hero video and fight the menu button.

It appears 2.2 seconds after load, so it does not ambush someone who
has not seen the page yet. There is a small x to dismiss it, which
matters: a widget you cannot close reads as spam.

Clicking opens a panel. Two setup fields, twelve questions one at a
time, the score, then the gate, then the plan.

The full page at /tools/gbp-check now gates the plan the same way, so
both entry points behave identically.


IMPORTANT: WHAT RESEND CAN AND CANNOT DO YET
--------------------------------------------
Resend's test sender can only deliver to YOUR OWN address. It cannot
email your prospects until a sending domain is verified.

So right now:
   You get the lead by email. Always. This is the part that matters.
   The prospect sees their plan ON SCREEN immediately.
   Nothing promises them an inbox delivery, because that would fail.

Once you verify send.thebizbuzz.studio and add RESEND_FROM, the
prospect email switches on automatically and the wording changes to
"a copy is on its way to your inbox". No code change needed.

The email to them is already written: their score, the band, all five
actions with reasons, and a soft pitch at the end.


THE LEAD EMAIL YOU RECEIVE
--------------------------
Subject: "GBP lead: Al Noor Dental scored 34/100"

Contains their score and band, all contact details they gave, their
five weakest areas, and a full table of every answer so you can see
exactly what is wrong before you reply.

Reply-to is set to their email, so hitting reply goes straight to them.


SPAM
----
A hidden honeypot field. Bots fill it, people never see it. If it is
filled, the submission is silently discarded and the plan still shows,
so a bot never learns it was caught. No captcha, because captchas cost
real submissions.


SETUP
-----
1. Sign up at resend.com with djfaraz@gmail.com
2. API Keys, Create API Key, name it bizbuzz-site, copy it
3. Open .env.local in C:\BizBuzz\thebizbuzz-studio and add:

      RESEND_API_KEY=re_your_key_here
      LEAD_INBOX=djfaraz@gmail.com

4. Restart: taskkill /F /IM node.exe, then npm run dev
5. Test the widget end to end and check your inbox
6. In Vercel: Settings, Environment Variables, add BOTH variables for
   Production, then redeploy

Step 6 is the one people forget. Without it the widget works locally
and silently fails on the live site.
