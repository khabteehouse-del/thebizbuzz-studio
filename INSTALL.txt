2. SEARCH METADATA
   This was the important one. Your page description said "A creative
   and technology studio building brands, products and AI systems for
   growing companies."

   That is the studio half only. It is also the exact text Google shows
   in search results and what appears when anyone shares the link. So a
   clinic owner finding you through search was reading copy written for
   startup founders, before ever reaching the page.

   Now: "We get local businesses found on Google, and we build the
   brands, websites and AI systems behind companies that are scaling up.
   Karachi and Dubai."

   It leads with the local half deliberately. That is the visitor most
   likely to arrive from a cold search rather than a referral.

3. TAGLINE REPETITION

   hero, footer heading, and the browser tab. Twice is a tagline, three
   times is repetition.

   The footer heading is now "Get found. Get built." which says what the
   two tracks do rather than repeating the line above it. The hero and
   the page title keep the tagline.
